2. How long we keep it

· Applicants who are not selected: deleted without delay after the draw for that round closes. If you chose “keep me in future draws”, we keep your data until you withdraw that choice or are selected.
· Selected applicants: kept for one year after the episode is completed, then deleted.
· Inquiries: kept until the inquiry has been resolved, then deleted unless further correspondence is needed.
· If you ask us to delete your data, contact us through the inquiry form. The delivery provider's separate retention period is described below.

3. How your photos and likeness are used

Your original photos never appear in the video. They are used only as reference for building the character. The character made from a selected applicant's face and body features, and the finished episode, may be published and promoted on YouTube, Instagram and other channels run by us or our partners. We ask for separate consent to this when you apply.

4. Sharing and processors

Website inquiries and applications are sent through FormSubmit (Devro LABS) to ceo@lecple.com. FormSubmit processes the fields and attachments to deliver the email. Its documentation states that text submissions are retained in its archive for 30 days and uploaded files are not retained in that archive. See the FormSubmit privacy policy and delivery documentation. Processing may take place outside your country.

If we switch applications to our Google Apps Script backend, application information and photos will instead be stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive, operated by Google LLC. The website's submission notice will identify that route.

Fit Room photos and body measurements are processed only in your browser and are not submitted through these forms.

5. Your rights

You may at any time ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop its processing. Send requests to the contact below and we will respond within 10 days. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 14.

6. Security

Only members of the production team with access rights can view application data. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS.
