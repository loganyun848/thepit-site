2. How long we keep it

· Applicants who are not selected: deleted without delay after the draw for that round closes. If you chose “keep me in future draws”, we keep your data until you withdraw that choice or are selected.
· Selected applicants: kept for one year after the episode is completed, then deleted.
· Inquiries: kept until the inquiry has been resolved, then deleted unless further correspondence is needed.
· If you ask us to delete your data, contact us through the inquiry form. The delivery provider's separate retention period is described below.

3. How your photos and likeness are used

Your original photos never appear in the video. They are used only as reference for building the character. The character made from a selected applicant's face and body features, and the finished episode, may be published and promoted on YouTube, Instagram and other channels run by us or our partners. We ask for separate consent to this when you apply.

4. Sharing and processors

Website inquiries and applications are sent through FormSubmit (Devro LABS) to ceo@lecple.com. FormSubmit processes the fields and attachments to deliver the email. Its documentation states that text submissions are retained in its archive for 30 days and uploaded files are not retained in that archive. See the FormSubmit privacy policy and delivery documentation. Processing may take place outside your country.

If we switch applications to our Google Apps Script backend, application information and photos will instead be stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive, operated by Google LLC. The website's submission notice will identify that route.

Fit Room · AI try-on and avatars

During photo upload and size comparison, photos, body measurements and usual sizes are processed only in browser memory. We do not infer body measurements or gender from your photo. Refreshing or closing the page clears your entries and generated results.

When the AI service is available, photos are sent only after you consent at the relevant stage and select Generate. Try-on generation uses your photo and the selected product image. Front-view avatar generation uses your original photo, try-on result and optional face reference. With separate consent, the same references and approved front image are sent sequentially to create three additional full-body views: left, back and right. With separate consent for 3D generation, the four approved images are sent to Meshy on fal.ai. Stopping generation prevents new requests; requests already submitted may continue processing.

Reference sheet assembly, 360° rotation, camera changes and .blend export run in your browser. Generated 3D files are downloaded through our server proxy. Models contain mesh geometry, UVs and textures; they are not body scans or motion data. Images are processed through Cloudflare Workers and fal.ai, using FASHN for try-on and FLUX.2 for avatar references. Height, weight, body circumferences and usual clothing sizes are not sent to these generation APIs. Generated images are not saved to a member profile or published in a public gallery.

Before transmission, photos are converted to JPG with a maximum dimension of 1,600 pixels, removing file metadata. We do not permanently store photos or results on our server. We disable input/output storage in fal.ai request logs and request a media expiry of one hour for images and two hours for 3D results where supported. Access to 3D jobs is restricted by a signed ticket valid for two hours, containing no photos or API keys. Downloaded files remain on your device. These measures do not mean that all third-party logs are immediately deleted. Additional processing and retention follow the fal.ai Privacy Policy and media retention documentation. Processing may take place outside your country.

We use Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated requests and excessive usage. Cloudflare may process your IP address and browser information for security checks. Our server stores a daily hash derived from your IP address, request identifiers and usage counts, and deletes them approximately 24 hours after the last permitted request. The original IP address is not stored in that usage database. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Choosing a different photo or product clears previous results. Download anything you want to keep before leaving the page. You can browse products and compare sizes without consenting to image generation. When you finish an avatar, an application form opens in the same tab. Original photos and generated avatar references are added to the form for your review. They are sent through FormSubmit to ceo@lecple.com only when you complete the application, give application consent and select Submit. Selected outfit information, height and clothing size shown in the form are included. The 3D model itself is not attached. Opening the form does not send an application or store a persistent draft.

5. Your rights

You may at any time ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop its processing. Send requests to the contact below and we will respond within 10 days. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 14.

6. Security

Only members of the production team with access rights can view application data. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS.
